04 · Your New Product

The kids' space-themed set

Pink space-bear t-shirt + green train shorts. Your first product that actually matches the Auranestt Kids brand. Here's how to launch it right.

This is the product to build the brand around. Stop being candles-plus-motorcycle-LEDs. Be a kids' brand.

✓ What's working in your photos

Clean white background, sharp focus, both front and back angles, natural colour. Good studio basics.

+ What you must add before listing

1. A child wearing the outfit (lifestyle shot — converts ~40% better)
2. Close-up of the space-bear graphic
3. Close-up of shorts detail (drawstring, train)
4. Folded "2-piece set" shot
5. Size chart graphic with measurements

Return-prevention built into the listing

Your 33% return rate is the thing that will kill this product. Prevent returns at the listing stage:

  1. Accurate size chart — chest width, length, shorts waist & length for every size. Most kids' apparel returns are sizing.
  2. Honest photos — show the real colour. The pink is fuchsia/hot pink; don't let filters make it look coral.
  3. Material clarity — state the fabric (cotton / cotton blend), GSM if known, wash care.
  4. Set expectations — "Pink t-shirt + green shorts as shown" so no one expects matching colours.
